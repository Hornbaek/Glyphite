    Sound studio

    Every song belongs to a project: the game it is for. Choose one to see its songs, or keep a sketch.

    Sound studio

    Compose music for the games in the style of the Super Nintendo: every instrument is a small sample with the console's grainy 4-bit compression, and the master has its echo. The sound comes from the engine itself, so a game plays a song exactly as you hear it here.

    Space
    play / stop
    R
    record into the selected track (a bar of count-in first)
    click, drag
    add a note; move it; drag its right end to change its length
    Shift + drag
    select several (Shift + click adds one)
    right-click, Del
    delete
    ↑ ↓
    move the selection a semitone (Shift: an octave)
    ← →
    move it by the grid
    Ctrl + C / V / D
    copy / paste at the start mark / duplicate after itself
    Ctrl + A
    select every note of the track
    Q
    snap the selection to the grid
    Ctrl + Z / Shift + Ctrl + Z
    undo / redo
    Ctrl + S
    save
    wheel
    scroll the notes (Shift: sideways, Ctrl: zoom)
    bar ruler
    click: where play starts; drag: the loop
    velocity lane
    drag up and down under a note to make it louder or softer
    VOLUME, PAN, FILTER, TEMPO
    the lane's menu: click to add a point, drag it, right-click deletes; the track (or the whole song, for tempo) follows the line as it plays
    [ MORE ] ▸ MIDI files
    open a .mid as a new song (each channel becomes a track, with its General MIDI instrument mapped to the nearest one here; channel 10 is the drum kit), or export the song as a .mid for another program
    instrument ▸ Load a sample…
    your own sound (WAV, MP3, OGG): set its note, loop and grit under SAMPLE
    WHERE IT PLAYS
    the places in the game the song is for; play the level with the song inside the game

    A MIDI keyboard plays the selected track (sustain pedal and pitch bend work). Without one, turn on KEYS and play the computer keyboard. Songs save to the site; a Claude session puts one into a game ([ MORE ] has the prompt).
