      Sound studio

      Compose music for the games in the style of the Super Nintendo: every instrument is a small sample with the console's grainy 4-bit compression, and the master has its echo. The sound comes from the engine itself, so a game plays a song exactly as you hear it here.

      the lane menu
      VOLUME, PAN, FILTER, PITCH BEND, the ECHO and REVERB sends, the LOOP POINT of a sample, and for the whole song TEMPO and the ECHO's level and feedback: click to add a point, drag it, right-click deletes; the track (or the song) follows the line as it plays. Shift + drag a stretch of the line bends it into a curve; Shift + click a tempo point makes the tempo glide to it
      [ MORE ] ▸ MIDI files
      open a .mid as a new song (you choose which instruments; each becomes a track, with its General MIDI instrument mapped to the nearest one here; channel 10 is the drum kit; volume, pan and pitch bend that move become lanes), or export the song as a .mid for another program (the song's own samples ride along inside it)
      instrument ▸ Load a sample…, Record a sample…, New multisample…
      your own sound (WAV, MP3, OGG, or the microphone): set its note, loop (start, end and crossfade) and grit under SAMPLE; a multisample plays several samples, each over its own range of notes
      BEATS ▸ [ + BEAT ]
      a beat is a pattern you make once: a clip of it goes into the selected track, and the beat opens in the editor. On a drum track it is a step grid: click a step to put a hit there, drag to paint or erase a run, Shift + click goes round soft, medium, hard; the menu by a row fills it (every 2nd, 4th…) or clears it; click a row's name to hear it. ROLL shows the same beat as notes (exact velocities, off-grid hits). Space plays just the beat, looped; Esc closes it. Every clip of a beat plays the same notes, so a change is heard everywhere it is used
      the overview (clips)
      drag a beat from the list onto a track to put a clip of it there; drag a clip to move it (Alt: a copy), its right edge to loop the beat (a notch shows each time round), its left edge to trim the start; Shift snaps to the grid instead of the bar, Shift + click selects several. Ctrl + E cuts at the start mark (or the playhead), Ctrl + D duplicates, Del deletes; right-click for the rest (Make unique, Turn into notes, Loop to the end…); double-click opens the beat
      [ FROM SELECTION ]
      select notes in the roll, and they become a beat (a clip plays it where they were: nothing sounds different). Double-click a clip in the overview to open its beat
      WHERE IT PLAYS
