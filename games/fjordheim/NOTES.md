## Start here

**The files** (`game.json` "parts" sets the order; they share one script scope with the engine):

| File | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `game.json` | title, description, controls, tunables (the editor's sliders), parts |
| `sprites.js` | generated by `tools/art.py`: every drawing (`FJ_ART`) and the ground tiles' colours (`FJ_GROUND`); never edit by hand |
| `world.js` | the valley: ground kinds and their materials, the tile grid (`ground`, `thing`, `ref`), trees, rocks and bushes, `genValley`, walking (`findPath`, A* over tiles) |
| `art.js` | `FJ`: the drawings as engine sprites, sized (`HEIGHT`, footprints) and anchored (`onFoot`); walk frames |
| `homestead.js` | `BUILDINGS` and their costs, `GOODS` (each good's icon), the stock, placing, finishing, pausing and tearing down, the day, seasons and what each dawn costs, nature growing back, fields, the knarr |

| `main.js` | the camera, mouse and touch (`tap`, `hoverAt`, `ghostFoot`, `PEND`), one step (`gameUpdate`), the news (`news`), light, drawing back to front (`gameDraw`), the HUD (the BUILD button, the build menu, the placing and building panels), `Glyphite.run` |
| `tools/art.py` | cuts the sheets in `ref/` into `ref/frames/` and `sprites.js` (see Art) |
| `tools/thumb_scene.js` | the scene for the game list's thumbnail |

**Probing** in a page (`page.evaluate`): `Glyphite.test.start(); Glyphite.test.freeze()`, then
`Glyphite.test.step(60)` steps a game second and returns the probe (season, day, stock, everyone's job, the buildings,
trees, hunger, cold, won, lost). Everything is a global: `HOME` (stock, buildings, time), `FOLK`, `BUILDINGS`,
`placeBuilding(type, i, j)` then `finishBuilding(o)`, `togglePause(o)`, `tearDown(o)`, `select(type)`, `tap(aim)`,
`centreOn([i, j, w, h])`, `isoToWorld`. The HUD's buttons are in `MENU` after a frame (`Glyphite.test.render()`), each
`{ id, c0, c1, r0, r1, act }` in HUD cells: the BUILD button is `'build'`, menu entries the building types, a building's
panel `'pause'`, `'raze'` and `'close'`.
Headless frames are slow (the 2D canvas up to a second): wait on game steps (`frameNo`), never on wall time.

**How the owner works**: they play on the live site, often on an Android phone, and send screenshots. Every change goes
out as its own pull request, merged once CI is green (`CLAUDE.md`, "Shipping changes"); then tell them what to try.

## Design

The owner's brief: "a small rts game ... inspired by anno 1404 and settlers 6. No fighting, just build a base and leave
peacefully with nature. Build a homestead for a new family in the Viking Age in Scandinavia", in an isometric view.

- **Player**: chooses what to build and where. Nobody is ordered about: like in The Settlers, the family hands out the
  work by itself (`assignJobs` in `folk.js`, every half second).
- **Goal**: reach the second spring (four seasons of `daysPerSeason` days of `dayLength` seconds: 8 minutes by default).
- **Lost**: three hungry dawns in a row (not enough food for everyone), or three cold winter nights (no longhouse, or no
  wood for its hearth). The family sails home.
- **World**: a 30 × 30 tile valley (`world.js`, `genValley`, seed 7, the next seed after a loss). The fjord runs along
  the lower left edge, a beach above it, meadow and grass, forest to the upper right, a rocky hillside at the top.
- **Look**: the owner's Gemini sheets, cut by `tools/art.py`. The ground is drawn in the back wall with thin texture
  glyphs; everything standing on it is a sprite, drawn back to front (`engine/12_iso.js`).

## Who does what

Priority, for each adult: building (a worker leaves the hut and goes back after) → an empty workplace (woodcutter,
fisher, sawyer) → the farmer (if there is a field) → breaking stone → resting. The child picks lingonberries in summer
and autumn, else plays.

| Job | Output |
|---|---|
| woodcutter | a log per tree (about 10 s with the walk); plants a tree for every two logs |
| fisher | rows out for 12 s: 2 food, 3 with a drying rack |
| sawyer | a log into a plank every 6 s; in autumn and winter leaves 6 logs for the fire |
| farmer | sows fields in spring and summer, reaps when ripe (`harvest` food); breaks stone in between |
| stone | a stone per trip from the nearest rock (boulders hold 8, small rocks 3) |
| child | a berry per bush visit (3 a bush each summer) |

**Pausing and tearing down** (a building's panel, `togglePause` / `tearDown` in `homestead.js`): a paused workplace
(woodcutter, fishing hut, sawpit, field) lets its worker go to other work and gets nobody until it is resumed; a fisher out
in the boat comes straight in, and whatever someone carries goes into the store (`quitJob`). With every field paused there
is no farmer. Tearing down gives back half the cost, rounded up (all of it for a site not yet finished: "call off"), frees
the tiles and lets go of the workers and the builder (who goes back to their own workplace); the tile grid's `ref` is
rewritten, since the buildings after it move down the list. It asks first: the first press turns the button red.

Buildings and costs are in `BUILDINGS` (`homestead.js`). The first newcomer comes by knarr when the longhouse has a free
bed and there are two days of food; later ones only to a homestead with a well. Food over `foodCap()` spoils (40, plus 60
per storehouse). The smithy makes all work a third faster.

## Balance (checked by `tests/playthrough.py`)

A sensible order (longhouse, woodcutter, fishing hut, two fields, sawpit, rack, a second woodcutter, well, storehouse,
smithy) ends the year with six people, every building done and no hungry or cold night; the forest at about 87% (the
deer need 85%). A player who builds nothing loses to the cold in the first winter. Starting food (16) lasts about six
days for three.

## Controls and the HUD
See `game.json` "controls" (shown on the game list).
- **The bottom** holds one thing at a time, opening upwards from the **BUILD** button (bottom left, `B`): the build menu
  (each building's own drawing as a small picture, `hudSprite`; its name; its cost as icons, red where the stock is short;
  what it is for, `short`), the building being placed (`[CANCEL]`), or the panel of a building that was clicked
  (`[PAUSE]`/`[RESUME]`, `[TEAR DOWN]`, `[X]`). Panels (`hudPanel`, `PANELS`) keep taps off the map; a tap beside the open
  menu closes it. Beside the button: what the mouse is over, or what was tapped. `1`-`9` still pick a building at once.
- **Icons**: every good has a glyph in its own colour (`GOODS`: `=` wood, `#` planks, `@` stone, `*` food; `&` folk,
  `^` forest): the stock on the top row (with names when there are 80 columns), the costs, and the "+1 WOOD" over a
  worker in the same colour. A paused building has an amber `||` over it.
- **The news** (`news(text, colour)`): up to four messages under the goal line, each for 6 to 10 s by its length, fading at
  the end; real time (not sped up by `[>>]`), held while paused. The same text again starts its time over. The engine's
  `say()` toast (1.6 s, one at a time) is left to the engine's own notices (detail level, running slow).
- **Picking**: `hoverAt` finds a person by their figure, else a building by its drawing (`buildingAt`: the painted pixels,
  frontmost first, with slack for a finger), since the roofs rise far above the footprints, else what is on the tile.
- **Mouse**: the building shows under the cursor and a click builds it; resting on a screen edge scrolls (`pointer()` in
  `main.js`, not `mouseAim()`, which forgets a cursor after 6 s still); right click or Esc cancels.
- **Touch** (`TOUCH()`: `Input.pointer`): there is no cursor, so the first tap on the map shows where the building would
  stand (`PEND`) and a second tap on the same spot builds it. Drag pans (a mouse can drag too). No on-screen arrows.
- Clicks and taps act on release (`'TapUp'`), so a drag never builds. The tap that starts the game is ignored on the map.
- The HUD's buttons (`hudButton`, `MENU`: rectangles in HUD cells): BUILD, the menu entries, `[>>]` (time x3) and `[?]`
  (help) on the top row, the panels' buttons. On a touch screen each takes taps most of a row and a column around it; the
  last row stays empty for the phone's gesture bar. Texts wrap to the screen (`wrapText`, `wrapBox`): a phone has 53 to
  56 columns, and the menu's two columns of entries need 53.

## Tunables
See `game.json` "tunables" (sliders in the web editor).
- `dayLength` 40 s: long enough to watch the work, short enough that a year takes 8 minutes.
- `daysPerSeason` 3: the winter is three nights, as many as it takes to lose.
- `startFood` 16: enough to build a hut for food before anyone is hungry, not enough to ignore food.
- `deerForest` 85%: two woodcutters all year thin the forest to about this.

## Art

`tools/art.py` (run it after changing a box): cuts the three sheets in `ref/` into `ref/frames/` (all 92 drawings, used
or not), writes `sprites.js` (the 70 used ones; characters share a palette per group) and `ref/ref.json` / `README.md`.
- Labels touching a drawing are masked with `drop` boxes (the smithy, the storehouse, the autumn birch, a deer, three
  fields): look at a contact sheet of `ref/frames/` after any change.
- `unpink`: the JPEG bled the raspberry background into the outlines; magenta-tinted palette colours become outline
  (dark) or transparent (light).
- `dense(pal, things=True)`: buildings, trees and goods get a dense glyph by material (thatch `%`, log walls `=`, stone
  `#` / `@`); people and animals keep `#`, `%`, `&`, `@`.
- `art.js` lifts every palette by a gamma of .8 and sizes sprites: people and trees by height (`HEIGHT`), buildings by
  their footprint's width.
- **Orientation** (`onFoot` in `art.js`): a building or field stands with its drawing's lowest point (`frontCorner`, its
  base's front corner) on the footprint's front corner (i + w, j + h). A footprint w along i (down-right on screen) and h
  along j (down-left) has that corner w / (w + h) of the way across, so a drawing whose own corner sits nearer
  h / (w + h) is mirrored: the longhouse (3 × 2) is drawn with its long side running down-left, and so is mirrored. The
  well is mirrored too; the rest stand as drawn.
- **The knarr** lies along the fjord (its drawing mirrored, bow to the upper left), so it sails parallel to the shore:
  in from the right, moored off the landing, out to the left (`shipLane`: three tiles off the wandering shore; nearer,
  its tall drawing stands on the beach). The rowboats row straight out, mirrored the same way.

## Decisions and known issues
- The world is static (no physics): the ground is the back wall, so lamps light it and nothing falls.
- Trees, rocks and buildings are cached sprites (`drawSprite` `cache`); people and animals are drawn fresh each frame.
- Not used yet from the sheets: the fence, goods (bread, firewood, iron, wool), the rams, the chopping block, the
  father chopping a log down, the black ravens. Sheep only wander.
- The ground at night and in winter is dim; buildings are readable but dark. A cell background colour in the engine
  would help both (roadmap, phase 4).
- No saving: a game is about 8 minutes.
- The storehouse stands on thin stilts that mostly vanish as glyphs, so it looks lifted; the knarr's tall sail rises over
  the beach behind it (it is moored three tiles out so its hull is on the water).
- Buildings placed side by side overlap on screen (their drawings are wider than their footprints, roofs overhang); the
  playthrough bot keeps a tile between them.
