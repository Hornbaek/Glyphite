# Regolith Lander: notes

The engine's first game and its reference: almost every engine feature started here. Land on the pads at eight
lunar sites; blast basalt for He-3; bring lamp posts and the x3 roof down.

## Files
| Part | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `materials.js` | regolith, basalt, bedrock, ice, pad metal, rubble, He-3 crystal, glass, lamp mast |
| `songs.js` | the soundtrack as base64 MIDI (generated by `tools/compose.py`; `.mid` copies in `music/`) |
| `level.js` | the eight sites: heightfield, craters, mesas, pads, caves, the x3 roof, masts and lamps, crystals, ice |
| `lander.js` | lander geometry, spring-damper legs, control, rockets, meteoroids, contacts and scoring, He-3 shards |
| `boulders.js` | round rigid bodies that roll, get pushed by blasts and can crush the lander |
| `lamps.js` | floodlamps as engine mounts riding their masts and roofs when they break off |
| `view.js` | lights, world drawing, HUD, title screen, the Earth on the sky layer |
| `lander_sprite.js`, `lander_art.js` | the pixel-art lander (from `ref/lander_ref.png` via `tools/art.py`) and its sprite setup |
| `music.js` | the music director, MIDI file loading and MIDI IN, chip meters |
| `main.js` | states, keys, camera, and the hooks handed to `Glyphite.run` |

## Physics to keep
Touchdowns up to 3.5 m/s land, from 4.5 m/s the gear collapses (`tests/dbg_legs.py 1|2|3`); the lander fits under
the x3 roof at every detail level (`tests/check_x3_clearance.py`); generated levels have no floating islands
(`tests/check_floating_islands.py`). History and lessons: `docs/HISTORY.md`, `docs/LESSONS.md`.
