# cargo-bay

What this map looks and feels like (for the person writing the briefs; the seed picture and the sub-palette carry the rest):

- Cargo Bay 1, the first sector: a long hold of olive and brown hull panels, rusty seams, hanging vines and moss, a red cable, hazard stripes.
- Dark and warm: a few orange lamps pool light on the floor, everything else falls into deep shadow.
- The mood is "abandoned but alive": small bright accents (cheese, lamp, screen) on muted metal.
