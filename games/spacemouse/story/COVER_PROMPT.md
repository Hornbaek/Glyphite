the title lettering "STAR WHISKERS" with the cheese wedge and orbit ring, the banner "THE HOLY COW", the colour palette

never scary.

Changes:

1. **The mouse wears a space suit.** Same mouse, same pose, same determined face and big round ears, standing on the cheese
   rock in front. The suit looks home-made: a soft, slightly baggy suit, patched in places and held together with strips of
   silver duct tape, a small orange air tank on its back, chunky boots and gloves. A round glass bubble helmet, with the ears
   inside the glass. Keep the red scarf, tied around the neck of the suit and blowing in the wind, so the mouse is still
   recognisable at a glance.

2. **The cat wears a space suit too.** Same orange tabby cat with green eyes and the same calm, slightly smug look, standing
   beside the mouse. Its suit is sleek and well made (cats have the better technology): fitted dark blue panels with brass
   fittings and a small glowing gauge on the chest, a tool belt, and a round glass helmet. Keep the blue cape over the
   shoulders.

3. **The army in the lower right is ordinary cats, not robots.** Replace the armoured robot soldiers with real cats of
   many colours and kinds: tabby, black, white, grey, ginger, calico, fluffy and short-haired, with soft fur and normal cat
   bodies. Each one wears a bulky metal mind-control helmet: a dome with a small antenna on top and a blinking red light in
   front. The red glow comes only from the helmets, not from the cats' eyes. Their eyes look glazed and sleepy, as if they are
   sleepwalking, and they march in stiff rows. They should read as victims to feel sorry for, not as monsters.

4. **The Holy Cow.** Keep her on the left with the halo, but make her look serene and wise, with a calm half-smile. Add a
   soft stream of glowing, milky starlight flowing up from behind her into the sky and becoming the Milky Way across the
   background.

5. **The ships.** Replace the spaceship at the upper left with a small home-made rocket: a clear plastic bottle as the body,
   a tin can as the cockpit, three little mismatched rocket motors, loose wires and plenty of silver tape, with a wobbly
   exhaust trail. Keep the other ships.

6. **The great robot cat fortress** on the right stays as it is: the castle with the giant mechanical cat head, red eyes and
   paw banner. Add a few big pipes running into its base, with milk visible through small round windows in them.

Do not add any other text. Keep the title spelled exactly "STAR WHISKERS" and "THE HOLY COW".
